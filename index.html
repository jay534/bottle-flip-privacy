📋 1. Introduction

Welcome to Bottle Flip Challenge ("we", "our", "us"), developed by Jayesh | Kinetic Arcade. This privacy policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

Our app is designed to be safe for all ages, including children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements for children, and Google Play's Families Policy.

By using our app, you agree to the terms outlined in this privacy policy.

👶 2. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

✅ Our app is designed to be safe for children of all ages and fully complies with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.

We take children's privacy very seriously. Here is what you need to know:

  • No personal information collection: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).
  • No account required: Our app does not require registration, login, or any personal data to play.
  • No behavioral tracking: We do not track, profile, or perform behavioral analysis on any users, including children.
  • No interest-based advertising: Our app does NOT use interest-based advertising or remarketing for any users. All ads served are child-safe and age-appropriate (rated "G" — General Audiences).
  • No social features for children: The app does not include social networking features, chat rooms, or any way for children to communicate with other users online.
  • Parental controls: Parents can clear all locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage in device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us immediately at the email address below. We will promptly delete any such data.

📊 3. Information We Collect

Our app is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Game Data (Local Only): High scores, achievements, game progress, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload this data to any server.
  • Player Names (Local Only): Names entered in multiplayer (Pass & Play) mode are stored locally and are never transmitted online.
🔒 We do NOT collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, photos, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information.

📢 4. Advertising

Our app uses Google AdMob, a Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDK, to display advertisements.

⚠️ Important: Our app is configured with child-directed treatment. This means:
  • Child-safe ads only: All ads are tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA compliance flag enabled).
  • Maximum ad content rating: "G" (General): Only family-friendly, age-appropriate ads are displayed.
  • No interest-based advertising: Ads are NOT personalized based on user behavior or browsing history.
  • No remarketing: We do not use remarketing or retargeting for any users.
  • No deceptive ads: Ads are clearly distinguishable from game content and are not designed to cause accidental clicks.
  • Rewarded ads only: We use opt-in rewarded video ads — users choose to watch ads in exchange for in-game rewards. No forced or disruptive ads.

Google AdMob may collect limited, non-personal device information (such as device type and operating system version) solely for ad serving purposes. For more details, see: Google Privacy Policy

💳 5. In-App Purchases

Our app offers optional in-app purchases (cosmetic bottle skins) through Google Play Billing. All transactions are processed securely through Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Parents/guardians can manage and restrict in-app purchases through Google Play's parental control settings.

💾 6. Data Storage & Security

All game-related data (scores, settings, achievements, player names) is stored locally on your device using browser local storage within the app. We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to any external servers.

Since all data is stored locally, it is protected by your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

🔗 7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Families Self-Certified Ads SDK) — For displaying child-safe advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — For processing in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party tracking services.

🔑 8. App Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required for loading advertisements from Google AdMob and processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to check network connectivity before loading ads.
  • BILLING: Required for Google Play in-app purchases.

We do not request access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive device permissions.

⚖️ 9. Your Rights

You (or your parent/guardian) have the following rights:

  • Delete data: Clear all game data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.
  • Opt out of ads: You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove app: Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data completely.
  • Parental controls: Parents can use Google Play's Family Link and parental controls to manage app usage and in-app purchases.
