🍾 Privacy Policy

Bottle Flip Challenge | Last Updated: April 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Bottle Flip Challenge ("we", "our", "us"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using our app, you agree to the terms outlined in this privacy policy.

2. Information We Collect

Our app is designed with your privacy in mind. Here's what we collect:

  • Game Data: High scores, achievements, game progress, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload this data to any server.
  • Player Names: Names entered in multiplayer (Pass & Play) mode are stored locally and are never transmitted online.
  • Advertising Data: Our app uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's privacy policy for details.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device information, ad interaction data, and use cookies or similar technologies. Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage

All game-related data (scores, settings, achievements, player names) is stored locally on your device using browser local storage. We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to any external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not require any personal information to play. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Permissions

Our app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required for loading advertisements and CDN resources.
  • Motion Sensors (Optional): For gyroscope-based tilt controls. This data is processed locally and never transmitted.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Since all game data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's own security measures.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Clear all game data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
